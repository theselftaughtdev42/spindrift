{#- One availability: whether `game_id` can be played on `platform`, and whether that is the way it is meant to be played. Always rendered as part of a row, and the row is what the cycle endpoint answers with: a click here can change two cells, because deciding on one platform un-decides another, and the cell that loses its marker is always another cell in this same row. Swapping the row covers both outcomes with one response rather than a special case. The button fills the cell, which is what makes the whole cell the click target rather than an inner control. -#}
{#- The `id` is what survives the swap: htmx restores focus to an element carrying the same id in the replacement markup, so cycling a cell with the keyboard leaves the keyboard on that cell rather than back at the top of the document. The platform's space becomes a hyphen, because an id may not contain one. `aria-pressed` stays the availability alone: an intended cell is an available one, and a two-valued attribute has no third value to spend on the state beyond it. -#}
